SpendShame — Expense Splitting Feature Direction

Which direction fits best for the hackathon?

A

💀 Shame Squad — Group Villain Mode

Users create a group (like a squad). Each member connects their bank or uploads receipts. The AI assigns each member a villain persona and roasts the group's combined spending. Members see each other's worst habits. Expenses get split with AI suggestions. The group has a shared leaderboard of "who spent the worst this month."

Pros

  • Deeply integrated with SpendShame theme
  • Unique hackathon differentiator
  • Social + viral potential

Cons

  • More complex multi-user state
  • Requires group invite flow
B

📄 Smart Receipt Splitter

User uploads a receipt or bill image (restaurant, grocery, trip). OCR agent extracts line items and total. User picks who to split with (enter names/emails). AI suggests fair splits (equal, by item, by percentage). Each person gets a breakdown. No bank connection needed — pure document-based.

Pros

  • Simple, focused, demo-able in 30 seconds
  • OCR + AI is impressive for judges
  • Works standalone

Cons

  • Less tied to SpendShame villain theme
  • One-off utility feel
C

🤝 Both — Split + Shame

Upload receipts or pull from your bank feed. Create a group. OCR extracts items from uploaded bills. AI villain roasts the group's combined expenses and suggests splits. Each member sees their share with a villain taunt. "You ordered the lobster. Of course you did."

Pros

  • Most feature-complete
  • Shows depth to judges
  • OCR + AI + social in one

Cons

  • Largest scope
  • Risk of half-finishing both
